06 / FAQ

Questions before we start?

Can you help if I’m not in IT?

I work mainly with IT and digital professionals, as well as leaders and business owners across sectors. Tell me about your situation and I’ll let you know whether I can help.

How is this different from coaching?

I draw on hiring experience and knowledge of the job market to explain how candidates are assessed and what you can improve. You remain in control of your career decisions.

What if I’m not sure what I need help with?

That’s fine. Turning a general feeling of dissatisfaction into a concrete challenge is part of the consultation.

Do you guarantee a job offer?

No. Outcomes depend on the market, your experience and your actions. I’m responsible for the quality of preparation and helping strengthen your positioning.

Can you help with English documents?

Yes. I can help you prepare written materials in English: CVs, LinkedIn profiles and cover letters for international applications.
